## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 (methodology_rigor).** I take this as the question of whether the evidence was generated and analysed in a way a methods referee in applied machine learning for health sensing would accept: whether the nesting of the data (participants, their recordings, and the windows cut from those recordings) is honoured both when the data are partitioned and when uncertainty is estimated; whether every data-dependent choice, including the representative data used to set post-training-quantization parameters, is kept away from the held-out evaluation set; whether confirmatory analyses are kept apart from exploratory or descriptive ones and the number of comparisons is accounted for; whether point estimates are accompanied by the spread attributable to training seeds, resampling draws, and participants, with effects judged against that spread rather than by whether an interval happens to exclude zero; whether each number agrees wherever it is repeated, from the abstract through tables and figures to any supplementary result files; and whether an outsider could rerun the pipeline from released code, pinned environments, and public data. The dimension is mandatory and assigned to my seat, so under the contract's failure rules a fatal finding here would alone force rejection and a block would alone force major revision; I therefore reserve fatal for flaws that remove the evidential basis of the primary claim and keep under-reporting at block or warn. Since the contract treats the work as a research prototype with clinical validity out of scope, I will not penalise the absence of clinical validation.

**D2 (domain_accuracy).** This dimension asks whether what is said about the application area is factually right: its terminology, its account of earlier studies and their findings, and its reading of the current evidence base. It belongs to the domain seat and I will not score it. Its only methodological edge is comparability, since a contrast with previously published figures means something only when the splits, label definitions, and metrics behind those figures match; if I notice such a protocol mismatch I will mention it as context, not as a score.

**D3 (argumentative_coherence).** I read this as whether the central thesis holds together and whether the conclusions are actually licensed by the evidence presented, free of reasoning errors that would undercut the main argument. The devil's-advocate seat owns it and my role is also eligible to score it; my contribution is inferential warrant, meaning I check that no claim is stronger than its design and statistics permit: confirmatory versus exploratory force, demonstrated equivalence versus mere non-significance, controlled versus confounded comparisons, and evaluated scope versus claimed scope.

**D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance).** This is about whether readers from neighbouring communities, for example health-informatics readers meeting quantization and on-device inference or embedded-systems readers meeting health time series, can follow the framing and definitions, and whether claims that cross disciplinary lines are backed by evidence. It is high priority and owned by the perspective seat, so I will not score it; its methodological edge is that statistical and quantization terms need definitions precise enough for such readers to interpret reported uncertainty and trade-offs correctly.

**D5 (writing_and_structure).** This covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains itself, the quality of its figures and tables, and whether it follows the venue's conventions. The editor-in-chief seat scores it and I will not; I will only point out presentation problems that obstruct verification of the methods, such as tables missing sample sizes at each level of the data, the type of interval shown, or units.

**D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution).** This asks whether the work belongs at the configured venue and whether it adds something original and significant for that venue's readers. It is mandatory and owned by the editor-in-chief seat, and I will not score it. From the methods side, the significance of an empirical contribution is capped by the robustness of its findings, so my D1 observations bear on this judgement only indirectly and the call on it stays with the editor.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Participant-disjoint (grouped) partitioning with participant, recording, and window counts per split; every data-dependent step (normalisation or imputation statistics, early stopping, model, configuration, or threshold selection, and post-training-quantization calibration/representative data) fit only on non-test participants, with calibration-set provenance and size stated in participants as well as windows; windowing and signal-derived targets that respect recording gaps and imputed samples; a fully specified quantization scheme (weight and activation precision, per-tensor vs per-channel, integer-only vs dynamic-range, operators left in float); float-versus-quantized and reference-versus-on-device comparisons paired on identical inputs, reporting paired differences, prediction agreement or flip rates, and per-class shifts; uncertainty estimated at the independent unit (participant/cluster bootstrap, mixed-effects, or GEE) with resample count, interval type, and seeds stated, plus training-seed and resampling-draw variability reported separately; a fixed, dated analysis plan (primary endpoint, decision rule, any equivalence margin) whose fixation demonstrably precedes access to evaluated outcomes, with confirmatory and exploratory analyses labelled and deviations disclosed; an explicit multiplicity account across sweep configurations, datasets, and metrics; imbalance-aware metrics (macro-averaged scores, per-class recall, confusion matrices) with per-class support and at least one trivial baseline; an on-device measurement protocol (hardware, OS, runtime and delegate versions, threads, warm-up, iteration count, percentiles, model-only vs end-to-end timing); and released code, pinned environments, data and model hashes, and scripts that regenerate every reported number so that abstract, text, tables, figures, and result files agree.
what_triggers_block: A repairable flaw that biases a reported result or misstates its precision: intervals or tests computed over windows or pooled samples as if independent when participants are the independent unit; normalisation, imputation, or feature statistics fit on data that include test participants; a data-dependent step (model or configuration selection, early stopping, quantization calibration) whose participant provenance relative to the test partition is undisclosed; headline effects resting on a single training seed or a single resampling draw with no seed or draw variability shown; multiple sweep configurations compared in confirmatory language with neither multiplicity control nor a pre-specified primary comparison; analyses labelled confirmatory or pre-registered that depart from the recorded plan (endpoint, margin, resampling unit, decision rule) without disclosure; or a headline number that cannot be traced to any table, figure, or released result file.
what_triggers_warn: Sound design with verification gaps unlikely to change any conclusion: correct resampling unit but missing resample count, interval type, stratification, or seed; a disclosed overlap confined to non-test partitions (e.g., training versus selection data) whose effect on test estimates is discussed; missing per-class support or trivial baseline; latency or memory figures lacking warm-up, iteration count, percentiles, or runtime/delegate details; exploratory analyses left unadjusted but clearly labelled as such; rounding-level mismatches across abstract, text, and tables; or regeneration scripts, pins, and hashes that cover most but not all reported results.
what_triggers_fatal: The primary quantitative claim loses its evidential basis: test-partition participants demonstrably contribute to fitting the weights, choosing the configuration, or setting the quantization parameters of the model whose test performance constitutes that claim, or float-versus-quantized (or reference-versus-device) results are compared on non-identical evaluation inputs, and no uncontaminated paired re-analysis is reported; or primary results stated in the text contradict, in direction, the manuscript's own released result files.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each claim in the abstract, contribution list, and conclusion is matched to evidence of adequate strength: confirmatory claims tied to pre-specified tests and exploratory findings framed as hypothesis-generating; 'no loss' or 'equivalent' statements backed by equivalence or non-inferiority analysis against a stated margin rather than by non-significance; effect sizes interpreted against training-seed, resampling-draw, and participant-level spread rather than interval sign alone; causal attribution of a performance change to one design factor (e.g., calibration-data composition, quantization scheme, window length, model size) supported by comparisons that hold the other factors fixed; conclusions drawn from the full set of evaluated datasets, metrics, and configurations rather than a favourable subset; generality limited to the datasets, populations, sensors, devices, runtimes, and quantization schemes actually evaluated; key terms (quantization calibration vs probability calibration, parity, equivalence) defined once and used consistently; agreement in direction and magnitude between abstract, body, and tables; limitations attached to the specific claims they bound; and no implied clinical-validity or treatment-guidance inference from retrospective benchmark evidence.
what_triggers_block: A central or abstract-level claim outruns its evidence in a repairable way: non-significance or overlapping intervals asserted as equivalence or 'no degradation' without a pre-specified margin; post-hoc or sweep-selected results stated with confirmatory force; a difference no larger than the seed-to-seed or draw-to-draw variability its interval omits, promoted to a headline finding because that interval excludes zero; a causal attribution to one design factor drawn from comparisons in which that factor co-varies with others; a general conclusion resting on a favourable subset of the evaluated datasets, metrics, or configurations; or abstract or conclusion figures whose direction or magnitude disagrees with the body or tables.
what_triggers_warn: The central thesis stands but secondary statements overreach: generalisation to unevaluated datasets, populations, devices, runtimes, or quantization schemes without hedging; causal verbs for descriptive sweep trends in the discussion; a key term used in shifting senses across sections; or limitations listed generically instead of being tied to the claims they qualify.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is refuted by the manuscript's own primary evidence (the primary estimate or its interval points opposite to the stated conclusion), or the core contribution depends on an inference the design cannot support at all, such as clinical validity or treatment guidance drawn from retrospective benchmark data.
