## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 (methodology_rigor; mandatory; scored by the methodology seat).** For the editor, this dimension settles whether the field could trust the study and rerun it from what is reported. In empirical health-sensing ML that means participant-level separation with no overlap among the data used for fitting, model selection, quantization calibration and final testing; uncertainty estimated at the level of the person or recording rather than the individual window, because windows from one wearer are not independent; honest handling of multiplicity and of the line between pre-specified and post-hoc analyses; and code, configurations, seeds and artifacts sufficient to regenerate the reported numbers. I do not score it, but I will make sure its verdict carries into the final decision rather than being diluted by favourable scores elsewhere.

**D2 (domain_accuracy; mandatory; scored by the domain seat).** This dimension asks whether the manuscript gets its subject matter right. Statements about the sensing modality and its measurement behaviour, any clinical or device conventions the work borrows, and prior results on quantization and on-device inference must be accurate, current and faithfully cited, and specialist terms must carry the meaning the domain gives them. Editorially, a domain error that changes what a claim means is a substantive defect rather than a copy-editing point, and I will treat it that way when reconciling the panel.

**D3 (argumentative_coherence; mandatory; owned by the devil's advocate and also open to the methodology seat).** This dimension checks that the paper argues one consistent thesis and that its results actually carry it. The claim made in the introduction should be the claim the evidence supports; conclusions should not outrun the data, for instance by treating an interval that merely excludes zero as a meaningful effect whatever its size against seed, draw and cluster variability, or by promoting exploratory findings to confirmatory standing; and no selective configuration choice, shifting baseline or internal contradiction should be doing the argumentative work. Because the dimension is mandatory, a single well-grounded block is enough to send the paper to major revision, so I will weigh the strength of the reasoning behind any such verdict rather than count votes.

**D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance; high priority; scored by the perspective seat).** This dimension asks whether readers from neighbouring communities, such as ML-systems engineers, health-informatics researchers and wearable-sensing practitioners, can follow the framing, definitions and implications without insider knowledge, and whether any step that crosses a disciplinary line, for example from a model metric to a statement about users, field deployment or care, is actually supported. A block here would by itself force a major revision, so I will expect that verdict to rest on identified passages rather than on general taste.

**D5 (writing_and_structure; normal priority; scored by me).** This dimension is about whether a careful reader can find each piece of evidence and read it correctly: a section order that follows the evidential chain, a visible boundary between pre-specified and exploratory results, tables and figures that stand on their own, numbers that agree wherever they recur, stable terminology, length proportionate to the contribution, and the reporting conventions this field expects. As a normal-priority dimension it can by itself move the outcome no further than minor revision, so I reserve block for presentation failures that would cause the evidence to be misread and warn for those that merely slow the reader down.

**D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution; mandatory; scored by me).** This is the editorial core: whether the submission belongs in front of an empirical applied-ML-systems and health-informatics readership and gives that readership something new and consequential, be it a finding, a method, a measurement protocol or a reusable artifact, positioned against the closest prior work, sized honestly to what was actually evaluated, and presented as a research prototype rather than a clinically validated tool. No venue-specific criteria are bound for this run, so fit will be judged only against those field norms, and I will make no claim of alignment with any named venue. Because the dimension is mandatory, a block here forces major revision and a fatal finding, meaning no salvageable original contribution or work outside the field altogether, forces rejection.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A section order that follows the evidential chain (data and participant-level splits, models and quantization, pre-specified results, exploratory or post-hoc analyses, limitations) with the confirmatory/exploratory boundary visible in headings, tables and captions; every number in the abstract, conclusions and contribution list matching the body text, tables, figures and any supplementary result files the text points to, at the stated precision; self-contained main tables and figures that identify the metric, split, resampling unit (participant versus window), seed and draw counts, interval type, float versus quantized variant and, for latency or size, the device, runtime and measurement protocol, keeping emulated and on-device numbers apart; per-class reporting where classes are imbalanced; honest axis scales; consistent names for model variants, classes and metrics; secondary sweeps placed in appendices; data, code, licence and intended-use statements where the field expects them; and prose that stays inside research-prototype framing.
what_triggers_block: A headline number in the abstract, conclusions or contribution list contradicts, or cannot be traced to, the table, figure or result file it summarizes beyond rounding at the stated precision; or confirmatory and post-hoc results are interleaved in the same tables or sections without labels, so a reader cannot tell which claims were pre-specified; or for a main result the resampling unit, interval type, seed/draw count, float-versus-quantized identity or emulated-versus-on-device provenance cannot be determined from the table or figure, its caption and the methods it cites; or a figure's truncated axis or mismatched panel scales visually exaggerate or hide a float-versus-quantized difference.
what_triggers_warn: Presentation defects that leave a careful reader's conclusions unchanged, such as rounding-level mismatches between sections, drifting names for variants, classes or metrics, captions that can be decoded only by consulting the methods section, sweep detail or repetition that belongs in an appendix, figures illegible at print size or reliant on colour alone, missing signposts such as an explicit contributions list or limitations section when that content exists elsewhere, absent or incomplete dataset licence and ethics statements, or isolated phrasing that drifts toward clinical implication without asserting it.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: One clearly stated primary contribution (empirical finding, method, measurement protocol or reusable artifact) that is new against the closest on-device, quantized and health time-series prior work, including any published baselines on the same data; significance argued in terms this readership acts on (paired float-versus-quantized deltas with participant-level uncertainty, model size, latency, memory) and large relative to seed, draw and cluster variability; a contribution carried by the pre-specified confirmatory analyses rather than by post-hoc sweeps; stated scope (datasets, model family, quantization scheme, hardware) matching what was evaluated; and framing as a research prototype with no clinical-validity claim, with fit judged against these field norms only because no venue criteria are bound for this run.
what_triggers_block: A genuine contribution exists but cannot be accepted as framed, because the closest prior on-device, quantized or health time-series work is missing so novelty cannot be checked, or the title, abstract or contribution list generalizes beyond the datasets, model family, quantization scheme or hardware actually evaluated, or the primary finding only re-confirms widely documented behaviour (such as small accuracy loss from 8-bit post-training quantization of small networks) without a new condition, explanation or protocol that would change a practitioner's choice, or the headline claim rests on post-hoc analyses presented as confirmatory or on an effect no larger than the reported seed, draw or cluster variability while being framed as substantive, or significance is argued through clinical utility that a research-prototype scope cannot support.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is original and correctly positioned and scoped, but its value to readers is blurred, because the primary claim is not separated from engineering deliverables, deployment implications (when reduced precision is an acceptable trade and at what size and latency cost) are left for the reader to infer, peripheral related-work strands are thin without weakening the novelty claim, or reusable artifacts that add to the contribution are not identified as such.
what_triggers_fatal: Nothing original survives revision, because every headline finding, method and artifact restates already-published results (including published baselines on the same data) or duplicates a separate prior or concurrent publication by the same authors (a preprint of this same manuscript does not count), or the submission lies outside empirical applied-ML-systems and health-informatics work altogether (no empirical model evaluation, as in a product or app description, or a contribution that exists only as a clinical-validity or treatment-guidance claim), so no revision could yield a contribution for this readership.
