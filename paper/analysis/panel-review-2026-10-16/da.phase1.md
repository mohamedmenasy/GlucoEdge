## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: For every headline claim (title, abstract, contribution statements, conclusion), build a claim-to-evidence map and test it on five axes: traceability (a specific table, figure or analysis supports it, and its numbers agree across abstract, body, tables, figures and cited result files); strength (stated magnitude, direction and certainty are licensed by effect sizes judged against seed, resampling-draw and subject- or cluster-level variability, not merely by an interval excluding zero); scope (claimed generality across populations, datasets, devices and operating conditions stays within what was sampled); provenance (thesis-carrying results were fixed before test outcomes were seen, exploratory, post-hoc and descriptive analyses are labeled and not recruited as confirmatory support, and multiplicity across configurations is acknowledged); and reasoning (no non-significance read as equivalence, no single configuration promoted to a general rule, no mechanism inferred from co-variation across configurations, no curated subset of baselines or metrics, no rival explanation such as leakage, within-subject dependence or confounding left open, and conceded limitations carried into the abstract and conclusion).
what_triggers_block: The central thesis is salvageable but is stated more strongly or broadly than its evidence allows, and repair needs re-analysis or substantive rescoping rather than rewording: a thesis-carrying result picked post hoc (best seed, configuration, threshold or split) is presented as confirmatory; hypotheses are reframed after the results to fit them; conclusions generalize beyond the evaluated datasets, subject pool, hardware or operating conditions; the comparison that carries the thesis omits obvious baselines, metrics or unfavorable configurations; a difference is called real or meaningful while it lies within seed, draw or cluster-level spread; headline framing implies clinical validity or real-world readiness that a research-prototype evaluation cannot support; or a numeric inconsistency across sections flips the direction or significance of a central claim.
what_triggers_warn: Localized coherence defects that leave the core thesis intact and are fixable by rewording, hedging or added reporting: a secondary claim or single sentence outruns its supporting result; analyses labeled exploratory in the methods are discussed in confirmatory language; a post-hoc explanation for an unexpected result is offered without being flagged as conjecture; limitations conceded in the body are not reflected in the abstract or conclusion; non-significance is loosely read as equivalence for a non-central comparison; or numbers disagree across sections without altering any conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The reported evidence itself contradicts or nullifies the central thesis, for instance the headline effect vanishes, reverses or becomes uninterpretable once any leakage, test-informed selection or within-subject dependence evident from the manuscript's own reporting is accounted for, or the core claim is logically incompatible with the manuscript's own tables, so that no rescoping or re-analysis of the existing evidence base would leave a defensible central contribution.
